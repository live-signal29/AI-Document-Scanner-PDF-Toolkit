## 2. Full Description (Rich Text for Google Play Store)

**AI PDF Scanner – Scan, OCR & PDF** by **FX Signal Lab** turns your Android device into a pocket document powerhouse. Designed for professionals, students, accountants, and remote teams, this scanner delivers crisp scans, instant on-device text recognition, and intelligent document summaries — with **100% offline privacy**.

### 🌟 WHY CHOOSE AI PDF SCANNER?
Unlike other cloud-dependent scanners that upload your personal documents to external servers, **AI PDF Scanner processes everything locally on your device**. Your sensitive receipts, legal contracts, business cards, and identity documents stay secure in your private storage.

### 🚀 KEY FEATURES

#### 📷 1. Ultra-Fast Multi-Page Camera Scanner
* **Real-Time Edge Detection:** Automatically isolates document borders with high-precision quadrilateral detection.
* **Auto-Perspective Correction:** Eliminates skewed angles, shadows, and perspective distortions.
* **Magic Color Enhancement:** Sharpens text, removes background yellowing, and optimizes readability.
* **Multi-Page Batch Mode:** Rapidly scan dozens of pages in seconds and compile them into a single PDF.
* **Filter Presets:** Magic Color, High-Contrast Black & White, Grayscale, and True Photo.

#### 🔤 2. On-Device OCR (Optical Character Recognition)
* **100% Offline & Private:** Powered by Google ML Kit Vision running entirely on your smartphone.
* **High-Accuracy Recognition:** Extract text from printed sheets, receipts, book pages, and contracts.
* **One-Tap Actions:** Copy text to clipboard, search within scans, or export to notes and messaging apps.

#### 🤖 3. AI Document Intelligence (Gemini Assistant)
* **Executive Summaries:** Turn 10-page documents into concise bullet-point executive briefs.
* **Entity Extraction:** Automatically detects critical dates, amounts, invoice totals, emails, and signatories.
* **Ask Document (Q&A):** Ask questions about complex contracts or bills and get instant answers.
* **Multi-Language Translation:** Seamlessly translate extracted document text into Spanish, French, German, and more.

#### 🛠️ 4. Professional PDF Utility Suite
* **PDF Compressor:** Shrink massive PDF files by up to 70% without sacrificing reading clarity.
* **PDF Merger:** Combine multiple separate PDF reports into a single organized document.
* **PDF to Image:** Export high-resolution JPG images of any PDF page.
* **Photo to PDF:** Select photos from your gallery and instantly generate clean PDF portfolios.
* **System Printing & Sharing:** Direct Android Print Service integration and universal share sheets.

#### 🔒 5. Privacy-First & Zero Tracking
* No mandatory account creation or cloud sync.
* Built-in Trash Bin with safe 30-day restore and one-tap permanent purge.
* Zero telemetry on your document contents.
